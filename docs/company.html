Open when you land

Most flights into Tirana arrive at hours no rental desk wants to work. Ours does. That is the whole reason the company is called what it is, and it is the one promise everything else here is built around.

We are a Tirana company with two offices ten minutes apart: one at the airport, one on Rruga Njazi Meka in the city. 19 cars, 18 of them automatic, each photographed and priced on its own page so you know which car you are getting before you arrive.

Albania rewards a car more than almost anywhere in Europe. The Riviera, the mountains in the north, the Ottoman towns in the south: none of them are a bus ride. That is what we are actually renting.

Straight answers

  1. Q1
    Are you really open at 4am?

    Yes. Airport pickups run around the clock, seven days, including the night flights from Istanbul and Rome. Tell us the flight number and the car is there when you clear customs.

  2. Q2
    Which licence do I need?

    An EU, UK or US licence is accepted as it is. Licences in other alphabets should come with an International Driving Permit. Bring the physical card, a photo of it is not enough at the desk.

  3. Q3
    Can I take the car to Kosovo, Montenegro or Greece?

    Cross-border travel is arranged case by case and needs the green card insurance document prepared in advance. Ask when you book, not on the day.

  4. Q4
    Is the price per day the final price?

    The daily rate covers the car, basic insurance and unlimited kilometres inside Albania. Longer rentals are cheaper per day. Ask for the total before you commit and we will send it in writing.

  5. Q5
    What about the roads?

    The SH1 and SH2 are motorway standard. The coast road over Llogara and the routes into the mountains are narrow, steep and worth every minute. Take a diesel automatic and do not book the smallest car for the passes.

  6. Q6
    Do you deliver the car to my hotel?

    Inside Tirana, yes, at no extra cost. Outside the city, ask and we will tell you honestly whether it is worth it.

  7. Q7
    Fuel policy?

    You get the car full and return it full. The station nearest both offices is open all night, like we are.

  8. Q8
    How do I actually book?

    Send the dates, the car and your flight number by WhatsApp or phone. You get a written confirmation the same day, usually within the hour.
