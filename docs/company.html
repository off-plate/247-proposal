Open when you land

Most flights into Tirana arrive at hours no rental desk wants to work. Ours does. That is the whole reason the company is called what it is, and it is the one promise everything else here is built around.

We are a Tirana company with two offices ten minutes apart: one at the airport, one on Rruga Njazi Meka in the city. 19 cars, 18 of them automatic, each photographed and priced on its own page so you know which car you are getting before you arrive.

Albania rewards a car more than almost anywhere in Europe. The Riviera, the mountains in the north, the Ottoman towns in the south: none of them are a bus ride. That is what we are actually renting.

What you get

  1. 01
    Actually 24 hours

    A 03:40 landing is a normal pickup time here, not a problem to solve at the desk. The name is the opening hours, not a slogan.

  2. 02
    The car in the photo

    Nineteen cars, each one photographed and priced on its own page. You book that car, not a category with 'or similar' printed next to it.

  3. 03
    Airport or city, same key

    Collect at arrivals, drop in town. Two locations, ten minutes apart, no fee for using both.

  4. 04
    Automatic as standard

    Eighteen of nineteen cars are automatic, which matters on Albanian mountain roads more than anywhere you have driven before.

  5. 05
    A person on the phone

    One number, answered by someone in Tirana who can move a car. Not a queue, not a chatbot.

Questions get straight answers

Licences, borders, night pickups, which car survives the road to Theth, and what the daily price actually covers. 20 of them, grouped and written plainly.
