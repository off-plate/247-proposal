Where people actually go

Eight places worth the drive, with the road time from Tirana and a car that can do it. Distances are one way.

Ksamil

Far south, past Sarandë

Three small islands close enough to swim to, in water that looks edited. Go in June or September: in August the road in is a car park and the beaches are full by nine.

280 km4 h 30 from Tirana
Volkswagen PassatLong motorway run, take the comfortable one.40 €/day

The Blue Eye

Between Sarandë and Gjirokastër

A spring nobody has found the bottom of, pushing cold water up through a pupil of dark blue. Park at the gate and walk the last kilometre. Swimming is officially discouraged and the water is about ten degrees anyway.

18 km20 min off the SH99
Hyundai TucsonThe last stretch is a rough track.50 €/day

Butrint

South of Sarandë

Greek, Roman, Venetian and Ottoman ruins stacked in a forest on a lagoon, and a UNESCO site since 1992. Two unhurried hours. Go early, before the tour buses arrive from Corfu.

18 km25 min from Sarandë
