Where to drive

Three routes worth the rental, then eight places people go. Road times are from Tirana one way unless the card says otherwise.

The Albanian Riviera

The road climbs to a thousand metres at Llogara, turns a corner, and the whole Ionian coast is suddenly below you. Then it drops through Dhërmi and Himarë to water that has no business being that colour. This is the drive people fly here for.

Route
Vlorë to Sarandë over Llogara
Length
120 km
Time
3 h driving, 6 h with stops
Best
May, June, September
Ask about this
Golf 6 Cabrio 2012The one drive that earns a roof you can drop.45 €/day

North to the Accursed Mountains

The SH1 runs fast and flat to Shkodër, then the country stands up. The final climb into Theth is a mountain pass with a valley of stone houses at the end of it. Ground clearance is not optional on this one.

Route
Tirana to Theth, via Shkodër
Length
250 km
Time
5 h
Best
June to September, closed in winter
Ask about this
Hyundai Tucson 2014The last hour to Theth is not a sedan road.50 €/day

Berat and Gjirokastër

Two Ottoman towns, both UNESCO listed, both built as stacked stone windows on a hillside. Easy roads the whole way, which makes this the trip to hand to someone who is nervous about driving abroad.

Route
Tirana to Berat to Gjirokastër
Length
230 km
Time
4 h
Best
April, May, October
Ask about this
Volkswagen Passat 2014Long, smooth kilometres. Take the comfortable boot.40 €/day

Ksamil

Three small islands close enough to swim to, in water that looks edited. Go in June or September: in August the road in is a car park and the beaches are full by nine.

Where
Far south, past Sarandë
Distance
280 km
Road time
4 h 30 from Tirana
Ask about this
Volkswagen Passat 2014Long motorway run, take the comfortable one.40 €/day

The Blue Eye

A spring nobody has found the bottom of, pushing cold water up through a pupil of dark blue. Park at the gate and walk the last kilometre. Swimming is officially discouraged and the water is about ten degrees anyway.

Where
Between Sarandë and Gjirokastër
Distance
18 km
Road time
20 min off the SH99
Ask about this
Hyundai Tucson 2014The last stretch is a rough track.50 €/day

Butrint

Greek, Roman, Venetian and Ottoman ruins stacked in a forest on a lagoon, and a UNESCO site since 1992. Two unhurried hours. Go early, before the tour buses arrive from Corfu.

Where
South of Sarandë
Distance
18 km
Road time
25 min from Sarandë
Ask about this
Audi A3 2014Short hop, easy to park.40 €/day

Gjirokastër

The stone city, built in slate and shuttered windows up a steep hillside under an enormous castle. The old town streets are cobbled and severe: park below and climb.

Where
South, inland
Distance
230 km
Road time
3 h 30 from Tirana, direct
Ask about this
Mercedes Benz c220 2013Hills all the way, and cobbles at the end.45 €/day

Lake Koman ferry

A working ferry through a flooded canyon that people compare to the fjords and Halong Bay, and for once the comparison holds. The car goes on the boat. Book the crossing ahead in summer.

Where
North, above Shkodër
Distance
150 km
Road time
2 h 30 to the ferry ramp
Ask about this
Hyundai Santa Fe 2013Seven seats, and it takes the ferry with you.60 €/day

Valbona valley

Alpine valley at the top of the country, and the start of the Valbona to Theth walk if you have a spare day. Snowed in through winter, so June to September only.

Where
Far north, the Accursed Mountains
Distance
250 km
Road time
5 h, or via the Koman ferry
Ask about this
Hyundai Tucson 2014Gravel and river crossings. Ground clearance matters.50 €/day

Krujë

Skanderbeg's castle above an Ottoman bazaar of carpets and copper. The easiest real day trip from Tirana, and the one to do on your first afternoon while you get used to the roads.

Where
Just north of Tirana
Distance
35 km
Road time
45 min
Ask about this
Golf 5 2005Half a day out. Take the cheapest key on the board.30 €/day

Shkodër and Rozafa

A castle on a ridge where three rivers and a lake meet, and the best sunset view in northern Albania. The city below is flat, green and full of bicycles.

North, on the lake
Distance

Road time
