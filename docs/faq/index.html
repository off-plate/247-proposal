Booking and paying

How do I book a car?

Send us the dates, the car you want and your flight number by WhatsApp or phone. We reply with a written confirmation on WhatsApp. There is no account to create.

Is there a deposit?

Any deposit is settled at the office when you collect, and nothing is charged in advance through this website. The amount, and whether it is cash or a card hold, depends on the car, so ask us when you send your dates and we will confirm it in writing with the total before you travel.

Do I pay online?

No. Nothing is charged in advance and no card details are taken through this website. You settle at pickup, once you have seen the car.

Is the price per day the final price?

The rates on this site are our daily rates, per car, in euro. Ask us for the total for your exact dates and we will send it to you in writing before you commit to anything.

Is it cheaper for a longer rental?

Usually yes. The daily rate comes down as the rental gets longer, so send us your dates and we will work out the total for you.

Can I change or cancel?

Plans move, especially with flights. Tell us as early as you can by WhatsApp and we will confirm the change in writing. Ask for the cancellation terms when you book so you have them from the start.

The cars

Is the car in the photo the car I get?

Yes. You book the exact car you see, by name. Every photograph on this site is of the car itself, taken at our office. If you would like a more recent photo, ask us and we will send it with your confirmation.

Are the cars automatic?

Nineteen of our twenty cars are automatic. The one manual is the Golf 5, which is also our cheapest car at 30 euro a day. On the mountain roads an automatic is worth having.

Do you have a seven-seater?

Yes, two of them, both Hyundai Santa Fe at 60 euro a day. They are a good choice for the Lake Koman ferry or the northern valleys with luggage.

Which car should I take for the mountains?

Take something with ground clearance. The Tucson or a Santa Fe handle the track into Theth and the last stretch to the Blue Eye. A low sedan is comfortable on the motorways and the coast road, though the rough final kilometres are hard work in one.

Can I add a second driver or a child seat?

Yes, both are common requests. Mention them in the same message as your dates and we will have them ready before you arrive, with the cost confirmed in writing.

Driving in Albania

Which driving licence do I need?

EU, UK and US licences are used here routinely. A licence printed in a non-Latin alphabet should come with an International Driving Permit, which you need to get in your own country before you travel. Please bring the physical card with you.

What are the roads like?

Better than their reputation. The SH1 north to Shkodër and the SH2 are motorway standard. The coast road over the Llogara pass and the routes into the north are narrow, steep, beautiful and slower than any map suggests, so allow extra time for them.

Can I take the car to Kosovo, Montenegro or Greece?

Crossing a border needs a green card insurance document prepared in advance, so please arrange it with us when you book rather than on the day. Ask us before you plan the route.

Is parking difficult in Tirana?

Central Tirana is busy and paid parking around the Blloku district fills up. Most hotels have an arrangement. Outside the capital, parking is rarely a problem at all.

Anything about driving here I should know?

Drive on the right. Headlights are expected outside towns, seatbelts are required, and the alcohol limit is effectively zero if you have held your licence under ten years. Expect livestock on rural roads outside the towns.

Pickup and return

Are you really open at 4am?

Yes. Both offices are staffed around the clock, every day of the week, which is where our name comes from. Night arrivals from Istanbul and Rome are an ordinary part of our week.

Where exactly do I meet you at the airport?

Send your flight number with the booking and the meeting point comes back in your written confirmation, along with the name of the person who will be there.

Can I collect at the airport and return in the city?

Yes. The two offices are about ten minutes apart and using both is normal. Say which one you want for the return when you book.

Can you deliver the car to my hotel?

Inside Tirana this is a common arrangement. Ask when you book and it is confirmed in writing with any cost stated up front.

How does fuel work?

Ask us about the fuel arrangement when you book and we will put it in your written confirmation, so it is agreed before you drive away. The fuel stations nearest both offices are open through the night.

Still have a question?

Send it to us. Both offices are staffed around the clock, and we are glad to help with dates, borders, or choosing the right car for your route.
